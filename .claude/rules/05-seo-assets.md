# SEO and Assets

## SEO

- Keep charset and viewport metadata present.
- Maintain a descriptive page title and meta description focused on Trùm Mía, sugarcane products, Nha Trang, and Khánh Hòa.
- Keep canonical URL, robots directive, Open Graph title/description/image/URL, and favicon references accurate when they are present.
- Update SEO copy when the visible brand positioning changes; do not leave outdated slogans or unsupported claims in metadata.

## Assets

- Store deployable assets inside the website directory, primarily under `assets/`.
- Use clear subfolders such as `assets/products/` and `assets/stores/`.
- Prefer real, correctly identified product and store photography.
- Do not use an unrelated image merely because its filename sounds appropriate.
- If a real image is unavailable, use a clearly marked placeholder and record what must be replaced before production.
- Keep image paths relative and verify that every referenced file exists.
- Optimize image dimensions and file sizes enough to keep mobile loading practical.

## Brand facts

- Treat product ingredients, nutritional benefits, store details, social handles, and claims such as `100%` as facts that require confirmation.
