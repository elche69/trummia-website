# Brand and Design System

## Brand direction

- Build for Trùm Mía, a local sugarcane brand associated with Nha Trang and Khánh Hòa.
- Preserve the brand idea: natural sweetness, regional identity, traditional value, and a friendly modern presentation.
- The website should feel simple, welcoming, premium, and recognizable rather than generic.
- Use the brand name consistently as `Trùm Mía` and keep the local positioning visible in key areas.

## Color palette

Use the logo-led palette as the default visual language:

- Dark green: `#1a4a1a` for header, dark sections, and strong backgrounds.
- Main green: `#2d7a2d` for buttons and accents.
- Light green: `#4caf50` for hover states and secondary accents.
- Gold: `#f5c518` for highlighted headings and primary calls to action.
- Light gold: `#ffe066` for highlights.
- Cream: `#fffde7` for light sections.
- White: `#ffffff` for contrast.
- Dark text: `#1a2a1a` for readable body copy.

Avoid introducing unrelated dominant colors without a clear brand reason.

## Typography

- Use `Nunito` for body text and interface copy.
- Use `Baloo 2` for large display headings and expressive accents.
- Load both fonts from Google Fonts when the page is rendered without a local font bundle.
- Keep headings expressive but readable on small screens.

## Visual treatment

- Use generous but controlled rounded corners and light shadows.
- Use subtle scroll-reveal animation and restrained hover scale on cards.
- Prefer inline SVG icons or the existing icon system; emoji may be used sparingly as a brand accent.
- Keep decorative choices subordinate to product, local story, and conversion actions.
