# Page Structure and Content

The site is a static one-page experience with this section order:

1. Header with logo and sticky navigation.
2. Hero with the brand promise and primary calls to action.
3. About section introducing the brand and its local story.
4. Products section showing the sugarcane product line.
5. Why Us section explaining the meaningful differences.
6. Stores section with physical locations and map links.
7. Social or purchase section directing customers to active channels.
8. Footer with brand, navigation, contact, and copyright details.

## Content principles

- Make the real product and the Trùm Mía brand the first-viewport focus.
- Connect the story to Nha Trang and Khánh Hòa without making unsupported claims.
- Prefer specific, truthful product descriptions over exaggerated health or quality promises.
- Keep calls to action clear: explore products, find a store, or contact/order through an active channel.
- Do not add placeholder social links, phone numbers, stores, or product claims as if they were verified facts.
- When a detail is unknown, mark it for confirmation instead of inventing it.

## Section expectations

- Hero: brand name or promise, short supporting copy, primary CTA, store CTA, and concise trust signals.
- About: origin, values, and a short explanation of how the brand relates to sugarcane.
- Products: product name, accurate description, suitable image, and an obvious route to order or learn more.
- Stores: complete address, opening hours, phone number, and a working map link for each published location.
- Social: show only channels that are real and maintained; every external link opens safely in a new tab.
- Footer: repeat essential brand and contact information without adding unsupported details.
